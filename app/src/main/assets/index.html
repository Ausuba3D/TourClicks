The app estimates daily premium hours. On a scheduled day, hours over 8 are overtime and eligible hours over 10 are penalty overtime. On a nonscheduled day, the first 8 worked hours are overtime and eligible hours after 8 are penalty overtime. An optional penalty-overtime exclusion window can be entered in Settings. Weekly 40-hour and 56-hour rules, local instructions, eligibility, and payroll adjustments can change the final result.
